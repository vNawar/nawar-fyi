## Excluded / not used

- BIOS / flashing / motherboard chip closeups: not strong for your public portfolio and too easy to misread out of context.
- Repeated server photos: kept the best-looking angles only.
- Office desk screenshots/photos with potentially private business data: excluded.
- Router/WireGuard screenshots with keys/IPs: used only after blurring sensitive areas.
- Full graduation document: not published clearly; only a blurred decorative version is used.
